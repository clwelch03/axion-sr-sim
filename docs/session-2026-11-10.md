## Toward Baryakhtar et al. Fig. 9 (spin-down vs time for several f), first test

Goal: reproduce Fig. 9 of Baryakhtar, Galanis, Lasenby & Simon, arXiv:2011.11646 (PDF added as
`docs/papers/2011.11646.pdf`, the version dated Feb 18, 2021), right panel: 10 M☉, μ = 2.5e-12 eV, so
α = GMμ/ħc³ = 0.187. Their conventions match ours: M_pl ≡ 1/√G, attractive quartic λ ≡ μ²/f² (Sec. II, before Eq. 10),
which is the quartic term of our cosine potential, so their f is our f_a.

Approach for small f (their "large self-coupling" regime, Sec. IV B 4): the plateau does not depend on f_a in our units,
and the hole evolves (M_Pl/f_a)² times more slowly than the plateau relaxes, so track the plateau as a changes (direct
Tsit5 evolution to 1e10 yr, ≈ 6e21 M, is not feasible). With κ = (f_a/M_Pl)² and inner (horizon) fluxes F_E, F_J in f_a²
units: da/dt = −κ (F_J − 2a F_E). Then all small-f curves are one curve shifted by (M_Pl/f)², which their figure shows
(the drop to 0.66 moves 1e2 → 1e6 → 2e9 yr for f = 5e14 → 5e12 → 1e11 GeV).
