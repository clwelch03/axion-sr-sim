## Toward Baryakhtar et al. Fig. 9 (spin-down vs time for several f), first test

Goal: reproduce Fig. 9 of Baryakhtar, Galanis, Lasenby & Simon, arXiv:2011.11646 (PDF added as
`docs/papers/2011.11646.pdf`, the version dated Feb 18, 2021), right panel: 10 M☉, μ = 2.5e-12 eV, so
α = GMμ/ħc³ = 0.187. Their conventions match ours: M_pl ≡ 1/√G, attractive quartic λ ≡ μ²/f² (Sec. II, before Eq. 10),
which is the quartic term of our cosine potential, so their f is our f_a.

Approach for small f (their "large self-coupling" regime, Sec. IV B 4): the plateau does not depend on f_a in our units,
and the hole evolves (M_Pl/f_a)² times more slowly than the plateau relaxes, so track the plateau as a changes (direct
Tsit5 evolution to 1e10 yr, ≈ 6e21 M, is not feasible). With κ = (f_a/M_Pl)² and inner (horizon) fluxes F_E, F_J in f_a²
units: da/dt = −κ (F_J − 2a F_E). Then all small-f curves are one curve shifted by (M_Pl/f)², which their figure shows
(the drop to 0.66 moves 1e2 → 1e6 → 2e9 yr for f = 5e14 → 5e12 → 1e11 GeV).

Energy/J conservation cannot catch a wrong-sign channel (the hole update uses the same flux the cloud loses); the
per-channel sign test can: energy into the hole must have the sign of ω_c(ω_c − m_c Ω_H).

Result (t = 1.5e9, 2.1e10, 2.9e10, 3.4e10): every channel has the right sign; the channel sum equals
outward_flux(solution, 1) to ≤ 5e-26. No sign error in the |322⟩ channel ([0,1], m = 2: extracting, ω − 2Ω_H = −0.29).

What moves the hole: the m = 0, l = 0 component q = [2,−1] (frequency 2ω₂₁₁ − ω₃₂₂ ≈ 0.290, below μM ≈ 0.295, so it
cannot escape to infinity) is absorbed: energy into the hole, zero J. Its amplitude ∝ A₂₁₁² A₃₂₂, so it switches on as
|322⟩ grows; it is the BH-absorbed partner of the 2×|211⟩ → |322⟩ transfer (the excess |322⟩ rate ∝ 𝒩₂₁₁² above).
Absorbing energy without J lowers a (da/dt part −5.2e-13 at 2.9e10), the spin drops below the |211⟩ threshold, and
|211⟩ is absorbed (ω₂₁₁ − Ω_H = +1.7e-5, da/dt part +8.6e-13), which raises a. Net a' > 0 because the threshold itself
rises: the hole's mass grows, α = α₀ M/M₀ grows, and ω₂₁₁M rises.

Checks of that picture:
- Threshold vs mass from the free run: a_th = 0.8771 at M/M₀ = 1 and 0.86941 at 0.98148, slope 0.415 per unit M/M₀.
  Predicted at the restart's final M/M₀ = 0.99546: 0.86941 + 0.415 × 0.01398 = 0.87521; recorded threshold 0.87528,
  a = 0.87508. So the spin-up (+0.0057) is the threshold moving with the mass.
- Keeping a on a threshold that moves by 0.415 dM needs |211⟩ absorption δ per m = 0 absorption E_X of
  δ/E_X = (2a/M + 0.415)/(1/(ω_ref M²) − 2a/M − 0.415) = 1.68 at the 2.9e10 state; the channel fluxes give 1.71
  (2.9e10) and 1.80 (3.4e10).
Conclusion: no bug found in the horizon fluxes; within this model (no GW emission) the spin-up is the expected
response. Not checked: whether the [2,−1] channel's amplitude is resolved (n_angular = 1, l = 0 only; mesh built for
a = 0.9), and the size of the 2×|211⟩ → |322⟩ rate against an independent calculation.
